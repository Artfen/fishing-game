Privacy

Slow Tide needs no account. It shows no ads, uses no cookies and no analytics, and doesn’t track you. Your game stays on your own device unless you choose cloud saves or playing together. This page says exactly what is kept, where, and for how long.

On your device

Everything the game needs to keep is kept in your browser’s own storage on your device (or, in the desktop app, in its data folder). None of it is sent anywhere by itself.

Your gameThe save (slow-tide-save), a copy from the start of each session (slow-tide-save-backup), and any save kept aside: one that couldn’t be read (slow-tide-save-unreadable), the game before a new one (slow-tide-save-before-new-game), or a very old one (slow-tide-save-v1). Your other games, if you keep more than one (slow-tide-slots, slow-tide-slot-2, slow-tide-slot-3). It holds what you’ve caught and found, your boats and gear, your journal, your settings and the name you chose.
LanguageThe language you chose for the game: English, Spanish or French (slow-tide-lang).
PhotographsThe pictures you take with the game’s camera, in the browser’s database (slow-tide-photos).
The game itselfA copy of the game’s files, so it plays offline (a slow-tide-… cache for each version), the pages it fetched while you played (slow-tide-runtime), and a note that it’s ready (slow-tide-offline-ready).
Cloud sign-inOnly if you sign in for cloud saves: your sign-in session and your email address (slow-tide-cloud).
Playing togetherOnly if you play together: the session’s code, whether you started it, and when, to rejoin it after a reload within ten minutes (slow-tide-session).
Friend codesA fingerprint of each friend’s gift code you’ve used (not the code itself), in your save, so each is used once.
The desktop appThe same, kept by the app; and copies of your saves as files in its saves folder (slow-tide-save.json and one for each other game), its update setting (settings.json) and a short log of its last update check (updates.log).

To remove it: start a new game or delete a game (Your games, on the title screen), delete photographs from the journal, or clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings (which removes all of it). Uninstalling the desktop app leaves its data folder; delete that folder to remove it.

Cloud saves (optional)

Only if you sign in, from the journal’s Your progress, to keep your game safe and carry it between devices.

WhatYour email address (to send you sign-in links, and to know your account), and your saved game: the same as in your browser (your first game; other games stay on your device). With it, when it was saved and a number that changes with each save.
WhereIn the game’s Supabase project (Supabase stores it for us), in [the Supabase project’s region, for example the EU (Frankfurt)]. Sign-in emails are sent by Supabase.
Who can see itOnly you, signed in (the database lets each player read only their own), and the people running the game, through Supabase’s dashboard.
How longUntil you delete it. In the journal, signed in: Download my cloud save gives you a copy; Delete my cloud data deletes your save and your account, email address and all, at once.
WhyBecause you asked for it: to keep your game and bring it to your other devices.

Playing together (optional)

Only when you start or join a session with a code.

WhatThe name you choose; where you are and what you’re in or doing; your catches and, if you choose to share them, your finds and what you sell; the quick calls you send; and, from whoever started the session, the time and weather of their game.
To whomThe others in that session (up to eight), through Supabase’s real-time service, while the session lasts. Anyone with the four-digit code can join it.
KeptNothing: it isn’t written to any database, and it ends when you leave.

The site, downloads and updates

The game’s pages and files come from its web host, [the host: Vercel, or wherever this copy is published]. Like any web server, it keeps a log of requests (your address on the internet, the time, what was asked for) to run and protect the service, for [how long the host keeps its logs]. The game loads nothing from any other site: its fonts, pictures and sounds come with it.

The desktop app’s installers download from the game’s releases on GitHub, which, like any download site, sees your address on the internet. When the app starts it asks the same releases whether there’s a new version (unless you’ve switched that off, in the journal); it sends nothing else, and nothing about your game.

Your rights

Under data protection law (the GDPR, and the UK GDPR) you can ask to see the data held about you, have it corrected, have it deleted, have its use limited, object to its use, and have a copy to take elsewhere. For cloud saves you can do most of that yourself, in the journal: download your save, or delete your save and your account. For anything else, write to [contact email address for privacy]. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

If you’re under 16, ask a parent or guardian before signing in for cloud saves.
